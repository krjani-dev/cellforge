# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project aims to be a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community.
Everyone participating in the project is expected to help create an environment
where people can collaborate safely and productively, regardless of experience
level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that support a healthy community include:

- Being respectful in technical and non-technical discussions
- Giving feedback that is specific, actionable, and professional
- Assuming good intent while still addressing problems directly
- Welcoming questions, different perspectives, and constructive disagreement
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than people

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory, demeaning, or exclusionary language or conduct
- Trolling, baiting, dogpiling, or sustained hostile arguments
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Repeatedly ignoring community boundaries or moderator instructions

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews
- Project documentation and other repository-maintained channels
- Public spaces when a person is representing the project or its community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, and other contributions that violate these standards.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

- Requesting a change in behavior
- Issuing a warning
- Temporarily restricting participation
- Removing content
- Banning a contributor from project spaces

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately by
emailing **devbyjani@gmail.com**. Include any relevant context, links,
screenshots, or timestamps that can help with review.

Reports will be handled as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.
Maintainers will review the facts, decide on an appropriate response, and aim
to protect the safety of the affected people and the broader community.

## Good-faith moderation

Moderation decisions will be made in good faith based on the available context
and the project's responsibility to keep collaboration safe and productive.
